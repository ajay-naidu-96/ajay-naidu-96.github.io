# 1. Idea Validation 

## Find Customers First, Then MVP

Do not build anything, just start interviewing potential customers and do not introduce your product that you're building, understand if it's a problem and then seek answers as to how they are currently solving the problem. Why this works? Instead of toiling away at some random techstack building something that you see as a problem you'll be understanding firsthand how they are currently solving the problem that you're trying to solve. See the pattern across potential users and then build an MVP
